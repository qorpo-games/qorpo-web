TAMITOS in October: stories, movement and practical parent support

Q
QORPO Studio20 October 2026 · 6 min read

TAMITOS is developing several ways into the same world: stories children can recognise, movement families can share and practical information parents can find again. The October website work brings these routes closer together, particularly through the Slovak parent hub. This update explains what those connections mean, while keeping available services separate from projects still in development.

A book, a game and a parent directory solve different problems. Putting the same characters on each does not automatically make them one useful experience. The connection becomes meaningful when a family can move from a story to a relevant next step without having to learn a new website or guess which information applies to them.

This is a product and website snapshot checked on 20 October 2026. It is not a claim that every TAMITOS project launched this month, and it does not turn internal development activity into sales, user growth or clinical outcomes. Those results need their own evidence.

A parent hub with a specific audience

The Slovak TAMITOS parent hub brings articles, starting points, a directory and community routes into the main website. Its implementation deliberately distinguishes Slovak community navigation from the English blog. That matters because a local support question is often tied to a country, language or administrative process.

An English reader looking for a story about emotions does not necessarily need Slovak guidance about local services. A Slovak parent seeking nearby help should not have to sort through material written for a different market. Localisation works best when it preserves the relevant task, rather than translating every menu into every language without considering its purpose.

The hub's source includes dedicated routes for a parent starting out, information sources, food-related questions, therapies, data and a directory. These are organisational routes into information. Their presence is not a promise that a directory entry is personally suitable, that every source is current, or that a parent should make a treatment decision from a web page.

Community needs a route back to trustworthy information

Questions from parents and editorial articles serve different purposes. A discussion can reveal what someone is struggling with in daily life. A sourced guide can explain a general question more carefully. The useful connection is between those two formats, with clear boundaries around personal experience and factual information.

The community route is described as moderated: submitted questions and experiences go through moderator review before publication. That is an important product boundary. It allows a community to welcome real questions without pretending that every answer is a professional assessment. Moderation itself still needs people, policy and consistent execution.

Consider an illustrative parent journey. Someone wants to make a first appointment but does not know what to ask. A starting guide can help them collect questions, a directory can help them investigate local contacts, and a discussion can provide context from other families. None of those steps should claim to determine the child's needs. This is a model journey, not a reported user study.

The book remains a distinct product

A story offers something a directory cannot: characters, a shared moment and room to talk. The TAMITOS book page is a place to inspect the actual product and available edition. Readers should check the language and format rather than assume that an English article means every physical edition is in English.

The second book is presented as a preorder. A preorder and an available first book should have different purchase wording. A supporter should be able to understand what is being ordered, what happens after printing and which details are confirmed. This update does not promise Christmas delivery or an unannounced language edition.

For QORPO, the interesting product question is how a story can remain recognisable across formats while each format does its own job. A page should help a reader decide whether they want the book, information about the series or a practical support route. Sending everyone through the same purchase message would miss those different intentions.

Move development becomes easier to inspect

The website also has code for a public Move development snapshot. It reads a defined set of TAMITOS repositories from the shared studio collector, checks the data and separates a fresh snapshot from an unavailable or stale one. That is a useful transparency mechanism, provided the numbers are read correctly.

A commit count tells you that recorded development changes exist. It does not tell you how many people installed the app, whether a particular feature passed a device test, or whether money reached a family. Code, release evidence and beneficiary reporting answer different questions. Mixing them would make an attractive dashboard less useful.

The Move product page is the better starting point for current availability and the app's actual purpose. Development notes can explain what the team is working on, but app store listings and the installed version determine what a user can access. Points should not be described as cash or guaranteed funded donations without a real funded mechanism.

Entertainment and support can share a world without sharing every rule

TAMITOS includes books, characters, animation and games, but a colourful identity should not erase important differences between them. A coming project needs development language. An available product needs accurate purchasing information. A support page needs clear sources and a way to understand its limitations.

That separation also helps international growth. An English article can introduce the TAMITOS world and direct a reader to the appropriate edition or product page. The Slovak forum can remain useful for the local families it is designed around. A global brand does not require every local resource to pretend it is universal.

The same discipline applies to visual material. The illustration accompanying this article is concept artwork using the TAMITOS character reference. It is not a screenshot of a working app, evidence of a published episode or the cover of a new book edition. Actual products and available interfaces should be inspected on their own pages.

What should the next update demonstrate?

A useful next update should follow tasks through to their result. Can a parent find a relevant route? Can a reader identify the right book edition? Can a Move user understand the installed version and where to get help? Those questions are more actionable than announcing that the ecosystem has become bigger.

For the studio, the measurement should match the task. Completed navigation to a relevant support page is different from a book order. A qualified app install is different from a development dashboard visit. No numerical uplift is claimed here because this article is not an analytics report.

You can follow the broader work through QORPO Studio Live, read how we distinguish a prototype from production evidence, and explore the studio's projects. For a family looking for a next step today, start with the actual TAMITOS product or parent page that matches the question.

Questions about this update

Is the parent forum an English service too?

The local parent hub discussed here is Slovak. The English blog remains a separate reading route. Do not assume that Slovak service information applies in another country.

Does development activity prove that a feature is released?

No. A source change, a test result and a public release are different pieces of evidence. This update describes the website structure and development transparency without treating every commit as an app release.

Where can I see the original information?

Use the linked TAMITOS pages for current products and the Slovak hub for local resources. This editorial update was also checked against the site's parent-hub, resource-routing and Move development collector implementation. It provides product context, not medical advice or a claim of measured business growth.
