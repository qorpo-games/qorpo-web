TAMITOS website update: 27 October to 25 November 2026

Q
QORPO Studio25 November 2026 · 6 min read

From 27 October to 25 November 2026, the clearest verified TAMITOS website changes concerned finding practical support, understanding the source of directory information and exploring the developing story world. This 30-day review covers selected published website work and resources. It does not treat a website update as a released television episode, a new mobile feature or evidence of user growth.

The dates are inclusive. We checked the current public pages and their source implementation, then matched the changes discussed below to dated releases within that window. Existing resources are identified separately from changes introduced during the period.

4 November: more precise directory choices

The Slovak service directory gained school and dental categories alongside its existing support categories. The current page offers region, category and social-service-type controls, with a search field for a place or provider name.

The useful change is the ability to ask a narrower question. A family looking for a school should not have to treat every support listing as the same kind of service. A reader looking for social rehabilitation needs a different category from someone looking for healthcare rehabilitation.

The public TAMITOS directory makes those distinctions visible. This is a directory feature, not a statement that TAMITOS has assessed every listed provider for every child's needs. The categories help organise a search; they do not decide suitability.

Source context belongs beside the result

The directory presents source information and separates imported public-register data from provider confirmation. Its card implementation can display source dates, confirmation status and the particular fields a provider has confirmed.

That is a practical distinction. A current name or address does not automatically establish current capacity. A school appearing in a statistical source is not proof that it is accepting a new pupil today. A dental listing is not proof of autism-specific experience.

The page says that the records represent services and branches rather than a count of organisations. That helps prevent a directory total from being mistaken for the number of distinct providers. We do not convert those totals into a claim about families helped or available appointments.

Coverage remains a separate question

The 4 November rollout also added an explicit coverage notice for school and dental sources that are still being populated. The source-health section can distinguish a successful import from a failed latest import that is retaining previous good data.

A selected filter and an empty result therefore need context. The useful next step may be to change the search, inspect source coverage or contact an identified provider. A blank category should not be interpreted automatically as proof that no relevant service exists in the real world.

This is also why the current page's dates matter. A directory combines information observed at different times. Readers should inspect the date attached to the result they intend to use, rather than assuming every field was confirmed together.

A model directory visit

Consider a hypothetical parent looking for a nearby school and a separate dental contact. Start with the school category and the relevant region. Open the original source or provider page for a candidate result, then ask the school directly about the question that matters to the family.

For the dental question, change the category rather than carrying the school search assumptions into the next list. Check the provider's current contact route and ask about the required accommodation. Do not infer an answer from the fact that a record exists.

This example is a proposed way to use the directory, not an account of a real family's visit or a professional recommendation. It shows the intended relationship between narrowing a search and confirming a practical next step.

4 to 6 November: new reading and family resources

The period also includes published Slovak and English article pairs. Among the 6 November English resources are a guide to choosing one shared book gift or two for siblings, a guide to agreeing board-game rules and a pause, and an explanation of how changing three shots changes an animated story's meaning.

These are useful for different moments. The sibling book-gift article helps frame a purchase and sharing decision. The board-game article concerns a familiar family situation. The editing article explains a creative process through a small example.

Publishing those resources does not establish how many readers used them or what happened afterwards. Their practical value must be assessed through the content and, where available, measured use. We make no conversion or ranking claim in this review.

15 November: the series page shows more of its world

The animated-series page received a looping hero video and a moving gallery of current artwork. The gallery uses images with a controlled height and intrinsic width so different compositions can remain visible rather than all being cropped into the same card shape.

A follow-up change the same day made the moving gallery images load eagerly. The reason is specific to a moving track: a card can enter view through animation, so leaving its image to a later loading decision can produce an unhelpful blank moment.

The current English series page is the public place to explore that presentation. Our gallery development article describes the implementation in more detail.

Motion choices in the gallery

The gallery source provides a hover pause and a reduced-motion alternative that disables the track animation and allows horizontal browsing. These are specific behaviors of the gallery, not a complete accessibility certification of the website.

They also do not establish the behavior of every other moving element on the page. The separate hero video should be assessed separately. A useful release review keeps the gallery's motion policy attached to the gallery instead of extending the result to the whole page by assumption.

For a reader, the purpose is to make the artwork easier to explore. For the team, it creates concrete checks: the images should load, the composition should remain intact and the alternative browsing state should still expose the content.

Published presentation and product availability differ

The series presentation is public; the series itself remains in development. A new trailer-style hero or refreshed render does not prove that completed episodes are available. This review does not announce an episode release date.

The same care applies to the wider TAMITOS world. Website information about a book, a game or Move should be read with the relevant product's own availability statement. An animation article explains a process; it does not establish that the process has reached a particular production milestone inside an unreleased episode.

Keeping those distinctions clear makes a progress update more useful. Readers can enjoy the current work without being asked to infer a launch from an attractive image.

Existing resources still support the journey

The book pages and reviewer information remain part of the website's wider reading journey. They are useful context, but we are not relabelling every existing resource as a new release within this 30-day window.

Our recent book-reading link explanation covers the route from a product page to practical guides. The reviewer-kit article explains the separate resource for independent assessment.

Together, those pages give a reader different next steps: explore the story, inspect an edition, look for a reading idea or evaluate the work for a collection. Their existence does not establish a sale, a recommendation or a partnership.

What this review establishes

The selected dated changes are the 4 November directory rollout and context improvements, published resources from 4 to 6 November, and the 15 November series-page refresh and image-loading adjustment. Current public pages were checked on 25 November.

The expected benefit is clearer information and fewer avoidable interruptions while browsing. That remains an intended benefit, not a measured business outcome. We do not infer impact from commit counts.

Explore TAMITOS, use the source dates when looking for support, and follow QORPO's studio progress for further updates. This review records a specific website period rather than promising the next product release.
