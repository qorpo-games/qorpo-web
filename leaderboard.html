How Community Points work

Simple by design.

STAKE

Tokens = points

Your stake sets your level (see the staking table), and each level earns a fixed band of Community Points per day. The more you stake, the higher your level and the higher your daily rate.

01
BONUS

APY is a bonus, not a boost

Lock in the APY pool and you earn extra $QORPO on top. It does not multiply your points. It just pays you more tokens for locking.

02
NO LOCK

Flexible still counts

Don't want a lock? The no-lock Power pool earns Community Points too. You stay liquid and still climb the board.

03

How this board works

You earn Community Points every day by your QORPO Power level and they add up over time. The board ranks wallets by their total accumulated points. Your points are kept only while you stake at least 10,000 $QORPO (Ethereum + BNB Chain combined); drop below that and they reset. Stake more to earn faster and climb.
