The wheel

What you can spinPreview

Small numbers, real balance. Most spins land 0–2, the middle rewards regulars, and 10 is the rare jackpot (~1 in 16). Average ≈ 2.6 points a spin — about 80 a month if you show up daily.

How it works

Three simple rules

01

Stake to unlock

Any active $QORPO stake (ETH or BNB) gives you one free spin per day. No stake, no spin.

02

Spin within 24h

Your daily spin expires 24 hours after it's granted. Use it or lose it — that's the whole point of coming back.

03

Points stack up

Every spin adds Community Points to your balance. They accumulate and rank you on the leaderboard.

Provably fair. Each day the server publishes a commitment hash of a secret seed. When you spin, the result is computed as keccak256(seed + your address + day) mapped onto the wheel — then the seed is revealed so anyone can recompute and verify the outcome wasn't rigged. The spin is free and pays only Community Points, so it isn't gambling.
