What you can spin

Small numbers, real balance. Most spins land on 0 to 2, the middle rewards regulars, and 10 is the rare jackpot (about 1 in 16). The average is about 2.6 points a spin, and higher stakers get more spins a day.

How it works

Three simple rules

STEP 01

Stake to unlock

Stake at least 10,000 $QORPO (ETH or BNB combined) to unlock daily spins. A higher QORPO Power level means more spins per day (1 to 5).

STEP 02

Your spins reset each day (UTC). Use them or lose them, that is the whole point of coming back.

STEP 03

Points stack up

FAIR

Provably fair, by design

1 . CommitSeed is locked

Each day the server publishes a commitment hash of a secret seed.

2 . SpinResult is computed

Your wallet signs, and the result is derived from the seed, your address, the day and the spin number.

3 . RevealAnyone can verify

The seed is revealed, and your result card links to the proof for your wallet and day.

Provably fair. Each day the server publishes a commitment hash of a secret seed. When you spin, the result is computed as sha256(seed : your address : day : spin#) mapped onto the wheel, then the seed is revealed so anyone can recompute and verify the outcome was not rigged. The spin is free and pays only Community Points, so it is not gambling.

Daily spin requires an active stake. Community Points are off-chain rewards. The spin is free: you never pay to spin.
