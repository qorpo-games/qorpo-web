How it works

One token, every chain

$QORPO is a LayerZero omnichain token. Bridging doesn't create a wrapped copy — it moves the real token, 1:1, with your wallet in control the whole way.

Native, not wrapped

The same $QORPO contract lives on both chains. Bridging burns on one side and mints 1:1 on the other — you always hold the real token.

LayerZero secured

Messages are carried by LayerZero's audited omnichain protocol. You sign one transaction; delivery to the destination completes in minutes.

Non-custodial

Connect MetaMask, OKX or WalletConnect. QORPO never holds your funds — the transfer runs entirely between your wallet and the on-chain contract.
