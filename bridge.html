How it works

Three steps, one signature.

1ConnectMetaMask, OKX or any EVM wallet. The bridge reads which chain you are on and sets the direction for you.
2Enter the amountThe exact amount you will receive and the LayerZero fee show before you confirm. Keep a little ETH or BNB for gas.
3Sign onceConfirm in your wallet. Track delivery on LayerZero Scan. Tokens arrive in the same wallet on the other chain.
Why would I bridge $QORPO?

Staking and the daily spin work on both chains, but exchanges and gas prices differ. Ethereum is where most $QORPO liquidity sits; BNB Chain is cheaper to transact on. Bridge to whichever side you use.

Is this a wrapped token?

No. $QORPO is a native LayerZero omnichain token (OFT). The contract address is identical on Ethereum and BNB Chain, and the supply is shared, so 1 $QORPO sent is 1 $QORPO received.

Why are the last decimals rounded?

LayerZero carries 6 shared decimals across chains. The bridge rounds your amount down to the nearest 0.000001 $QORPO before sending, so nothing can get stuck as dust.

What if it says the transaction failed?

Almost always one of two things: not enough $QORPO on the chain you are sending from, or not enough ETH or BNB for gas. Flip the direction if your tokens are on the other chain.
