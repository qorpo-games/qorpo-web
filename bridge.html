How the bridge works

Three simple steps. Your $QORPO never changes value — it just moves to the chain you choose.

STEP 1

Connect & choose

Connect your wallet, pick the direction (ETH → BSC or BSC → ETH) and enter the amount.

STEP 2

Approve & confirm

Approve $QORPO once, then confirm the bridge transaction in your wallet.

STEP 3

Receive on the other chain

In a few minutes the same amount of $QORPO lands in your wallet on the destination chain.

Bridge FAQ

Which chains can I bridge between?

Ethereum and BNB Chain (BSC). Use the swap button in the middle of the card to flip the direction. The contract addresses and network switch are handled automatically per chain.

Is the amount the same on both sides?

Yes. Bridging is 1:1 — you receive the same amount of $QORPO on the destination chain, minus a small bridge fee that covers the cross-chain messaging. There is no price slippage.

How long does it take?

Usually about 3–5 minutes, depending on network congestion on the source chain. You can leave the page after confirming; the tokens arrive on-chain regardless.

What do I need in my wallet?

The $QORPO you want to bridge, plus a little native gas token (ETH on Ethereum, BNB on BNB Chain) to pay for the approve and bridge transactions.
