What is QORPO, and what is the $QORPO token?

RB
Rastislav BakalaCEO, QORPO · updated 8 October 2026
@RastislavBakala

QORPO is a game studio that builds in public. $QORPO is the single token that sits behind every game the studio ships. This page explains both in plain words: what the studio makes, what the token does today, where it trades and how you can check every claim yourself.

Short answer

QORPO is an independent game studio from Slovakia, building since 2020 with zero hacks. $QORPO (ticker QORPO) is its token, live on Ethereum and BNB Chain as one LayerZero omnichain token and listed on KuCoin, Gate and MEXC. Game revenue is used to buy $QORPO back from the market, staking earns daily Community Points, and every commit the studio makes shows on qorpo.world in real time.

A studio, not a single-game token

Most gaming tokens belong to one game. If that game stalls, the token stalls. QORPO is built differently: one small team, several products, and one token across all of them.

  • Citizen Conflict, a third-person shooter built in Unreal Engine 5 before the AI era. The technology base for everything that followed.
  • AneeMate, a creature collecting adventure for PC: catch, raise and become creatures on a living island. Free on Epic Games Store during Early Access. $QORPO is its main token.
  • One Life, an AI-native shooter built on Citizen Conflict tech by an experienced team, in public, with every build landing on the homepage.
  • Tamitos, our own children's IP about emotions and neurodiversity: an animated series, books, games and the TAMITOS Move app.

What the $QORPO token does today

  • Buyback engine. Revenue from the games is used to buy $QORPO back from the open market. Part is burned, part funds the next games. Details on the token page.
  • Staking. Stake $QORPO for daily Community Points by your QORPO Power level, plus APY tiers up to 30% paid in $QORPO from the on-chain contract. Stake here.
  • Daily spin. Stakers get a free, provably fair daily spin for Community Points. Spin page.
  • Leaderboard. Community Points rank you on the live leaderboard.
  • Airdrop eligibility. Holding or staking $QORPO keeps you eligible for the $TAMITOS airdrop tied to the animated series.
  • One currency. The same token works across QORPO games and collectibles.

Why "builds in public" matters

Anyone can say they are building. We show it. The live page and the popup on every page pull real commits from the studio's GitHub repositories: AneeMate, One Life and Tamitos, every author, updated hourly. In the last 30 days that was more than 1,000 commits from 7 builders. You do not have to trust a roadmap, you can watch the work land.

Where $QORPO lives

$QORPO is a LayerZero omnichain token (OFT). It exists on Ethereum and BNB Chain as the same token with the same contract address, 0x22514ffb0d7232a56f0c24090e7b68f179faa940, and can be moved 1:1 between them with the QORPO Bridge. It trades on KuCoin, Gate and MEXC. Market data is on CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap. New here? Read how to buy $QORPO.

Is $QORPO worth a look?

That is your call. What we can say factually: $QORPO trades at a micro-cap with a shipping studio behind it, three exchange listings, live staking and a public commit feed. That mismatch between what is built and what the market prices is exactly what people hunting small gaming tokens look for. It is also a gaming and AI token in a year where both narratives get attention. Micro-caps are high risk. Do your own research and only use money you can afford to lose.

Questions people ask

What is $QORPO in one sentence?

The token of QORPO, an independent game studio from Slovakia: one token behind AneeMate, One Life, Citizen Conflict and Tamitos, with buybacks funded by game revenue, staking rewards and a public commit feed.

Is QORPO a game studio or a crypto project?

A game studio first. It has shipped games since 2020 and uses one token, $QORPO, across its products. The token is the way to back the whole studio rather than one game.

How does the buyback work?

Revenue from QORPO games is used to buy $QORPO back from the open market. Part of it is burned, part funds the next games. It is a mechanism tied to real product revenue, not a price promise.

Where can I buy $QORPO and on which chains?

On KuCoin, Gate and MEXC against USDT. On chain it lives on Ethereum and BNB Chain as the same LayerZero token, bridgeable 1:1 on qorpo.world/bridge.

How do I check the studio is actually building?

Open qorpo.world/live. It shows commits from the studio repositories in real time, with counts for the last 30 days and all time, updated every hour from GitHub.
