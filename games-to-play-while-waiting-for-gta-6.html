Games to play while waiting for GTA 6

RB
Rastislav BakalaCEO, QORPO · updated 8 October 2026
@RastislavBakala

GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series. There is no PC date, which means PC players wait longest. Here is what fills the gap, split by what you are actually craving from GTA: open world chaos, a living world, or a game that respects your time and your wallet. QORPO has no affiliation with Rockstar.

Short answer

While waiting for GTA 6, console players can replay GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 or try Cyberpunk 2077 and Saints Row. PC players, who get no GTA 6 at launch, have more time: Cyberpunk 2077, Red Dead Redemption 2, Watch Dogs 2, and free picks like AneeMate, a creature collecting adventure on a living island that is free on Epic during Early Access.

If you want open world chaos

  • GTA 5 and GTA Online. Still the biggest sandbox, with a decade of content. Replaying it before GTA 6 is the obvious move.
  • Saints Row (2022). Sillier and lighter, with co-op from the first mission.
  • Watch Dogs 2. San Francisco, hacking instead of guns, and a lot of GTA's structure.

If you want a living world

  • Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar's other masterpiece and the best preview of what GTA 6's world will feel like.
  • Cyberpunk 2077. After its updates, the densest city in games and a story GTA will have to beat.
  • AneeMate. A different kind of living world: an island where day, night and rain change which creatures come out, where you build a base and play as what you catch. Free on Epic during Early Access on PC, and new builds land every week. The creatures you own are yours on chain, which GTA Online will never give you. Built by us at QORPO, so judge it on the free download.

If you want to own what you earn

Everything you grind in GTA Online belongs to Rockstar. Rockstar also bans crypto and NFTs on fan servers, so that will not change with GTA 6. If the idea of owning your progress appeals to you, web3 games are the place: AneeMate, Illuvium and Axie Infinity record what you own on a blockchain. We wrote about what GTA will not let you own.

The PC wait

Rockstar usually brings GTA to PC a year or more after consoles. GTA 5 took 19 months. That is a long time to wait, and a good reason to pick games that get better while you play them. AneeMate updates weekly and you can see every change land on the studio's live page.

Questions people ask

When does GTA 6 come out on PC?

There is no confirmed PC date. GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Previous GTA games came to PC a year or more later.

What free games are like GTA?

For open world chaos, GTA Online is free with GTA 5. For a free living world on PC with a different loop, AneeMate on Epic.

Will GTA 6 have crypto?

No. GTA 6 has no cryptocurrency and Rockstar bans crypto and NFTs on fan servers.

Is AneeMate related to GTA?

No. AneeMate is a creature collecting adventure by QORPO, an independent studio with no affiliation with Rockstar.
