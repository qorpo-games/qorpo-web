How it works

Non-custodial by design

Nobody holds your tokens but you. Every stake, unstake and reward is a transaction you sign, on contracts anyone can verify.

Your keys, your tokens

Connect MetaMask, OKX or WalletConnect. QORPO never takes custody — the contract holds the stake, and only your wallet can unstake it.

Two ways to earn

APY staking pays a fixed rate for a locked term. Power staking is flexible with no lock, building multipliers up to 25× for ecosystem rewards.

Audited & battle-tested
