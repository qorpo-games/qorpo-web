What you earn

Two rewards, one simple rule

Staking earns you Community Points every day. The more you stake, the higher your level and the more points you earn daily (see the table above). Lock in the APY pool and you also earn extra $QORPO on top.

CP

Community Points = your stake

Every staked $QORPO is one Community Point. That's the whole formula. See where you rank on the Community Points leaderboard.

APY

APY is a bonus

The APY pool pays extra $QORPO for locking your tokens for a fixed term. It doesn't multiply your points. It just pays more tokens.

KEEP

Points stay with you

Loyalty Community Points accrue over time and remain even after you unstake. (Daily accrual is tracked off-chain and rolls out with the points service.)

FAQ

Staking questions

How does QORPO staking work?

Stake $QORPO here, flexible or locked. Every staked token earns Community Points, and you can earn APY paid in more $QORPO straight from the on-chain contract.

What are Community Points?

A loyalty score that ranks you on the leaderboard. Your stake earns points every day, and the daily amount grows with your QORPO Power level. They are off-chain rewards, not a financial return.

What is the daily Spin?

A free, provably-fair daily reward for wallets staking at least 10,000 $QORPO. Higher QORPO Power levels unlock more spins per day. Rewards are Community Points, not money.

What APY does QORPO staking pay?

APY tiers pay additional $QORPO, roughly 15% (Low) to 30% (High), set on-chain by the staking contract and subject to change.

Is QORPO staking custodial?

No. Staking is non-custodial through your own wallet; QORPO never holds your private keys or funds.

Community

Staking uses real smart contracts and real tokens. Locked Holder stakes cannot be withdrawn early without forfeiting the APY bonus and paying an on-chain early-exit penalty. APY rates are set by the staking contract and can change; nothing on this page is a promise of returns or financial advice. Community Points are off-chain loyalty rewards, not a financial return.
