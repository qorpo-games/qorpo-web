Staking FAQ

What is the difference between Power and APY staking?

Power staking (no APY) gives you a multiplier that boosts your rank and ecosystem benefits. It can be flexible (No Lock, 20x, withdraw any time) or locked for a fixed multiplier: 90 days 22x, 180 days 23x, 360 days 25x. APY staking pays a yield in $QORPO instead, with Low (15%) and High (30%) tiers.

Can I withdraw a locked stake early?

Flexible Power (No Lock) can be withdrawn any time. Locked Power and APY stakes are held for their duration; unstaking an APY stake before it ends applies the penalty defined on-chain, so you receive your base amount minus the penalty. Once a stake has ended you can claim your amount plus profit.

Which chains are supported?

ETH Chain and BSC Chain. Switch the network at the top of the page; your wallet will be asked to switch to the matching chain. The staking contract address updates automatically per chain.

How does this keep me eligible for the $TAMITOS airdrop?

Holding or staking any amount of $QORPO keeps you eligible. The more you hold or stake, the bigger the potential. Check the Tamitos website for details.
