How to buy $QORPO

RB
Rastislav BakalaCEO, QORPO · updated 8 October 2026
@RastislavBakala

$QORPO is the token behind every game QORPO ships. Buying it takes about ten minutes on a major exchange. Here is the whole process in four steps, then how to hold it yourself and put it to work. Educational, not financial advice.

Short answer

Create an account on KuCoin, Gate or MEXC, fund it with USDT, open the QORPO/USDT market and buy. To really own it, withdraw to MetaMask or OKX on BNB Chain (cheap gas) or Ethereum (deepest liquidity), then stake on qorpo.world/staking for daily Community Points.

Step 1: pick an exchange

$QORPO is listed on three centralized exchanges. Create an account and complete verification on whichever you prefer.

Step 2: get USDT

All $QORPO pairs are QORPO/USDT, so fund your account with USDT. Deposit it from another wallet or buy it with a card or bank transfer on the exchange.

Step 3: swap USDT for $QORPO

Open the QORPO/USDT market and place a market order (instant) or a limit order (your target price). That is it, you are a holder.

Step 4: hold it yourself and stake

To truly own it, withdraw $QORPO to your own wallet (MetaMask or OKX) on Ethereum or BNB Chain. Then stake it for daily Community Points and APY instead of leaving it idle.

Which network should you use?

$QORPO is a LayerZero omnichain token, so it is the same token on both chains:

  • BNB Chain: lower gas fees, cheaper to move and stake small amounts.
  • Ethereum: deepest liquidity and the canonical contract.
  • Bridge any time: move between chains 1:1 with the QORPO Bridge. No wrapped tokens.

Always check the contract address before you add the token to a wallet: 0x22514ffb0d7232a56f0c24090e7b68f179faa940, identical on both chains.

After you buy: put it to work

  • Stake for daily Community Points by your QORPO Power level, plus APY tiers up to 30%.
  • Spin the free daily wheel once you stake, for more Community Points.
  • Climb the Community Points leaderboard.
  • Stay eligible for the $TAMITOS animated series airdrop.
  • Watch the studio ship on the live page. That is what you are backing.

Questions people ask

Where can I buy $QORPO?

On KuCoin, Gate or MEXC. Fund with USDT and buy on the QORPO/USDT market.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

Not to buy, you can hold on the exchange. To self-custody, stake or use $QORPO on chain, withdraw it to a wallet like MetaMask or OKX.

Which network is cheaper?

BNB Chain generally has lower fees than Ethereum. $QORPO is the same token on both and bridges 1:1 on qorpo.world/bridge.

How much should I buy?

$QORPO trades at a fraction of a cent, so you can start small. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Is there a minimum to stake?

Staking starts at 100 $QORPO. Community Points are retained while you keep at least 10,000 $QORPO staked. Full rules are on the staking page.
