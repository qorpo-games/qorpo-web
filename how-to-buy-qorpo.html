How to Buy $QORPO — Step-by-Step

Updated 31 August 2026 · by QORPO

$QORPO is the attention-economy token behind QORPO's on-chain games and the Tamitos animated series. Buying it takes about ten minutes on a major exchange. Here's the whole process in four steps — plus how to self-custody and put it to work. This is educational, not financial advice.

1

Pick an exchange

$QORPO is listed on three centralized exchanges — MEXC, KuCoin and Gate. Create an account and complete verification on whichever you prefer.

2

Get USDT

Most $QORPO pairs are QORPO/USDT, so fund your account with USDT — either deposit it from another wallet or buy it with card/bank on the exchange.

3

Swap USDT for $QORPO

Open the QORPO/USDT market and place a market order (instant) or a limit order (your target price) to buy $QORPO. That's it — you're a holder.

4

Self-custody & stake

To truly own it, withdraw $QORPO to your own wallet (MetaMask, OKX) on Ethereum or BNB Chain. You can bridge 1:1 between the two, and stake $QORPO for daily Community Points and APY instead of leaving it idle.

Why $QORPO → Stake after buying

Which network should you use?

$QORPO is a LayerZero omnichain token, so it exists on both chains as the same token:

  • BNB Chain — lower gas fees, cheaper to move and stake small amounts.
  • Ethereum — deepest liquidity and the canonical contract (0x2251…a940).
  • Bridge anytime — move your $QORPO between chains 1:1 with the QORPO Bridge, so you're never stuck on one network.

After you buy: put it to work

Holding is fine, but $QORPO has real utility. Once you own some, you can:

  • Stake for daily Community Points by your QORPO Power level, plus APY tiers up to 30%.
  • Unlock a free daily Spin once you stake at least 10,000 $QORPO.
  • Climb the Community Points leaderboard.
  • Stay eligible for the $TAMITOS animated-series airdrop.
