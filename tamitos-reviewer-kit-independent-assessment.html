Inside the TAMITOS reviewer kit: facts, artwork and independent judgement

Q
QORPO Studio24 November 2026 · 5 min read

The TAMITOS reviewer kit brings together book details, artwork references, edition information and suggested reading questions for someone making their own assessment. It is prepared by TAMITOS, the project behind the book. It is not an independent review, and receiving the information does not commit a librarian, educator or reviewer to a recommendation.

That distinction is part of the resource itself. A useful publisher pack should make facts easier to check while leaving the recipient free to decide what the work is worth, who it suits and whether to cover it at all.

Start with the affiliation

The public English reviewer kit identifies its publisher connection near the beginning. It also states that selection, evaluation and publication remain with the recipient.

This gives the information an appropriate starting point. A first-party resource can provide the official synopsis, author names and approved contact route. It cannot make the resulting opinion independent merely by placing a review-oriented label on the page.

For a model library workflow, use the pack to identify the title and edition, then apply the library's own collection policy. This is an original example of using the resource, not a claim that a particular library has selected the book.

Keep the edition attached to the facts

A book's title is not always enough to identify the product someone is considering. Language, format and edition identifiers belong together. A catalogue description for one edition should not silently become a description of every format.

The kit separates the Slovak book information from the English edition information. Before ordering or publishing bibliographic details, check the relevant catalogue and selected seller again. A dated information pack cannot establish today's stock, price or delivery options.

This is especially useful when a recipient is preparing a short listing. Copy the identifiers for the edition under discussion rather than assembling a line from several product pages. Our book-page navigation article describes the separate reader-facing guides that help families decide what they need.

Ask what each source establishes

The pack explains the roles of its sources. An official book presentation supports a description of how TAMITOS presents the story. A retailer catalogue supports edition details. An interview can document what someone said and the context in which it was published.

Those are different kinds of evidence. Catalogue presence is not a professional review. A media interview is not a clinical evaluation. A project's own impact page is not an independent reconciliation of every charitable payment.

Keeping these roles visible makes the pack more useful to an editor. A reviewer can ask a precise follow-up question instead of accepting every link as support for the same broad claim.

Reading prompts are suggestions

The kit includes questions about pictures, different experiences and asking whether someone wants company or quiet. It labels them as original suggestions for the pack, rather than quotations from the book or a clinically validated programme.

A recipient can therefore assess the prompts as conversation ideas. They do not need to treat them as a required script or test of a child's understanding. A child can listen, point to a picture or decline to answer.

For a model shared-reading session, an adult might choose one picture and one optional question, then follow the child's interest. That example illustrates a possible use of the resource; it does not promise a therapeutic result.

Public artwork still needs a reuse decision

The page includes official cover, illustration and portrait references. Its reuse note asks recipients to request written permission and exact credits before republishing images, extended excerpts or photographs. The pack does not grant an open licence.

The practical step is to identify the particular image and intended use when contacting the publisher. A review thumbnail, a printed event poster and a full illustration spread are different uses. Do not assume that being able to download a file settles the reuse question.

The kit gives a public business contact for routing those requests. It does not confirm that every recipient is entitled to a complimentary copy. Any copy arrangement remains a separate conversation.

A review can stay critical

The pack explicitly does not ask for a positive review, a prescribed ranking or an AI endorsement. It also distinguishes a factual correction from a change in editorial opinion.

For a model review process, the publisher might correct an edition identifier while the reviewer retains a reservation about the story's pacing. Those are compatible outcomes. Checking a fact need not turn the rest of a review into approved marketing copy.

If a copy or other consideration is supplied, the pack asks recipients to disclose it in their own output. It does not claim that this article has secured a review, a placement or a new partnership.

A small preparation checklist

First, identify the edition. Next, read the source-role notes and separate official descriptions from independent evidence. Inspect the actual sample before deciding whether to cover the story. If artwork will be reused, resolve permission and credit for that specific use.

Finally, keep publication status current. A prepared pack can outlive an offer or a product milestone. Recheck the live product page before repeating a preorder, dispatch or availability statement.
